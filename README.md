### Selected work

- **[Hype Out, Infrastructure In: Crypto's Partnership Rotation](https://thetie.io/insights/hype-out-infrastructure-in-cryptos-partnership-rotation)** (The Tie, 2026). A published study of 5,203 digital-asset partnership announcements. A BTC-adjusted event study against matched placebo windows shows they lift tweet volume, not price, and only for tokens under $2B market cap.
- **[Stock returns prediction with XGBoost and FinBERT sentiment](https://scholarship.claremont.edu/cmc_theses/2715)** (senior thesis, CMC, 2021). Next-day return forecasts from technical, fundamental, macro and tweet-sentiment features, validated with blocked time-series CV and explained with SHAP.
- **[Deep learning on the Black-Scholes model](https://github.com/samuellee19/CSCI145_Option_Pricing)** (CMC). A neural network trained on synthetic Black-Scholes prices and tested on real UKX and S&P 500 options (R² 0.87 and 0.88).
